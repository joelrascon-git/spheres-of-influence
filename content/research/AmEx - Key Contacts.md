- **Who to target first.** AmEx has publicly said it has "scaled AI-assisted development tools to more than 11,000 engineering professionals… and [is] scaling agentic coding tools" ([FY2025 Annual Report](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/4962/000110465926034161/tm265890d2_ars.pdf)). Grosfield, who leads "the AI transformation program strategy for the enterprise" ([AmEx IR](https://ir.americanexpress.com/governance-and-corporate-responsibility/executive-committee-and-directors/default.aspx)), is the top-down sponsor.
- **The wedge.** The best-documented pains map directly onto Factory's products:
  - a spec-driven, full-lifecycle AI SDLC that AmEx is already designing ([Formica, May 20, 2026](https://americanexpress.io/reimagining-software-delivery-with-ai/)), which fits Specification Mode, Missions and Automated QA;

  - the Oct 8, 2026 OCC/Fed BSA/AML consent orders, which fit Automations and Factory Private. Coverage of the OCC action cites inadequate resources, systemic internal control gaps and weak independent testing, and the orders set hard 90-day plan deadlines ([Daybreak Wire](https://daybreakwire.com/business/amex-350-million-aml-fine-consent-orders/); [Zyphe](https://www.zyphe.com/resources/news/american-express-aml-penalty-occ-october-2026)).
- **How to navigate.** Multi-thread through three unit CIOs:
  - Devand: U.S. Consumer Services, Platinum refresh.
  - McGowan: Global Commercial Services, next-gen Amex Corporate / Center / Hyper.
  - Kensey: Global Servicing & Corporate Technology, multi-LLM GenAI.

  Run in parallel with Security (CISO Gleb Reznik) and Data (CDO Chris Gifford). Respect the AI Transformation Office's risk and governance "approval pathways," which are being redesigned right now.
